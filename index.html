Dodge Viper ACR

The Pinnacle of 1st Gen American Muscle

The 1999 Dodge Viper ACR (American Club Racer) was built for the track, featuring lighter weight and a stiffer suspension setup. Powered by an 8.0L V10 engine, it delivers explosive acceleration and raw driving experience.

Specifications

  • Engine: 8.0L V10
  • Max Power: Approx. 460 hp
  • 0-100 km/h: ~4 seconds
  • Transmission: 6-speed manual
  • Production Years: 1999 – 2002

Key Features

  • Lightweight aluminum wheels
  • Racing-tuned suspension
  • Removed unnecessary features (A/C, audio, etc.)
  • Aggressive and aerodynamic design
